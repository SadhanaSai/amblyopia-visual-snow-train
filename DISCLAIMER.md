# Disclaimer

This is not a medical device, and using it does not create a doctor-patient relationship of any kind.

## Not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment

This app is a personal vision-training and self-tracking tool. The exercises and measurements are informed by peer-reviewed vision-science and neuro-optometry research (see the in-app Guide tab for citations), but that does not make them a substitute for evaluation, diagnosis, or treatment by a licensed optometrist, ophthalmologist, or other qualified clinician. Nothing in this app should be read as a specific recommendation for your own eyes, vision, or health.

## Not a clinical instrument

The visual acuity, contrast sensitivity, stereoacuity, and suppression measurements in this app are screen-based, uncalibrated against clinical equipment, and dependent on your own device, display, lighting, and self-reported viewing distance. They may be useful for spotting your own trends over time, but they are not validated for diagnosis and should not be used to rule in or rule out any condition. A clinician-measured baseline is recommended before starting, and periodically thereafter.

## Use at your own risk

Some exercises use flashing, flickering, or high-contrast stimuli. Stop immediately and consult a clinician if you experience double vision, headache, nausea, dizziness, eye strain, or any worsening of symptoms. If you have a history of photosensitive epilepsy, seizures, recent eye surgery, or any condition your clinician has told you to be cautious about, talk to them before using this app, and follow their guidance over anything presented here.
